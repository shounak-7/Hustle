Terms of Service & Community Standards

To maintain the integrity of our community and ensure safety for everyone involved, all users agree to the following terms:

  • ✓ Identity Verification: Every gig worker on Hustle undergoes identity verification and credential reviews prior to taking active bookings.
  • ✓ Pricing Integrity: Quoted prices on Hustle include standard labor costs. Any supplementary materials or unexpected scope changes must be mutually approved before execution.
  • ✓ Worker Dignity & Independence: Gig workers are independent professionals who set their schedules and manage their work. Customers agree to provide safe, harassment-free workspaces.
  • ✓ Cancellation Policy: Free cancellations are supported up to 2 hours prior to scheduled arrival. Cancellations made after a pro is en route incur a modest travel compensation fee.
  • ✓ Hustle Trust Guarantee: If a job does not meet quality standards, Hustle support facilitates prompt re-work or dispute resolution with customer support available 7 days a week.
